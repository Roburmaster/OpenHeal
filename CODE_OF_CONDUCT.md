# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

OpenHeal welcomes constructive participation from users and contributors. Project spaces must remain professional, technically focused, and safe to use.

Expected behavior includes:

- discussing ideas and defects respectfully;
- giving actionable technical feedback;
- accepting correction when evidence contradicts an assumption;
- respecting privacy and confidentiality;
- focusing criticism on code, behavior, and decisions rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, discrimination, or targeted insults;
- sexualized language or unwanted attention;
- publishing another person's private information;
- deliberately disruptive, deceptive, or malicious contributions;
- repeated off-topic arguments after a maintainer has redirected the discussion.

## Enforcement

The repository owner may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is harmful to the project or its participants.

Reports should be sent privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal details.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, release channels, and other spaces where a person is representing OpenHeal.

## Attribution

This policy is a concise project-specific conduct standard informed by common open-source community practices.
